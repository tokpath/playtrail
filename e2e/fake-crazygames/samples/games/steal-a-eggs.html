n entirden,Steal Eggs is an arcade collect game where your role is to steal eggs and run quickly to home base before you’re caught! Fierce animals act as guards so you will need to learn to run as fast as you can to get past them.

How to Play Steal Eggs

Your first tasks as a thief will be fairly simple. Head out of your base and find the nearest egg. Grab it by pressing E and run it back to your fenced-in plot before the guard animals catch you. The guard animals at first will likely not outrun you, unless you just get nervous and stop in your tracks so you should be safe but all you have to do is look around you and see speeding tigers chasing other players on the board. That will soon be you!

Once you get your egg, drop it in your pen and let it grow. When it hatches, you get a pet that earns money for you every second. This is the whole point of stealing the eggs - they make you passive money. So, keep stealing. Pricier eggs hatch into better pets, and better pets mean more cash. Spend that cash on upgrades, a bigger plot and more speed.You can buy your upgrades at the local shops, and when you are equipped enough with speed and protection, venture out for the rare stuff.

Steal It and Run

Every egg has a guard, and they don’t like thieves. Grab the egg and get moving, because one slow step and you’re getting chased. Watch out for traps and other players, too. Somebody else always wants the same egg you do.

Hatch Pets, Stack Cash

Back at your base, eggs grow until they’re ready to hatch. Some take a minute, some take hours, and the long waits usually mean something good is coming. Pets range from common to mythic, with bigger sizes and special variants like golden and rainbow. Fill your pen with your best animals and watch the money roll in.

Train on the Treadmill

Once you’ve got some cash, buy a treadmill and start training. Every upgrade boosts your speed, and speed is everything here. The faster you run, the easier it is to outrun guards and reach the tougher zones where the expensive eggs are.
